1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

Traditional municipal infrastructure monitoring relies on manual road inspections, sporadic complaint tickets, or expensive dedicated survey vehicles equipped with specialized LIDAR and laser profilers. These approaches are cost-prohibitive, intermittent, and incapable of providing real-time city-wide coverage.

The NovaFlow Paradigm: Rather than deploying new mobile assets or streaming terabytes of uncompressed video to cloud servers, NovaFlow converts existing municipal public bus fleets into continuous, real-time sensing networks. Every bus automatically inspects road conditions, monitors congestion, and detects safety hazards while servicing regular passenger routes.
⚡ Edge-First AI

Inference executes directly on on-vehicle edge accelerators (NVIDIA Jetson / Raspberry Pi 5 + Coral). Only 1–2 KB JSON event payloads and brief clips are transmitted, eliminating 99.8% of mobile bandwidth costs.

📡 Store & Forward

Zero dependency on continuous internet. Built-in transactional SQLite queues spool detections during cellular blackouts (tunnels, underpasses) and automatically drain upon 4G/5G reconnection.

🔒 Privacy by Design

Automated real-time facial and license plate blurring at the camera level. Ephemeral 30s rolling buffer ensures raw video is destroyed unless explicitly triggered by an incident.

3. The 4 Specialized Edge AI Vision Engines

The on-vehicle compute node executes four decoupled analytical vision pipelines concurrently. Each engine runs optimized TensorRT/ONNX models calibrated for edge inference:

🕳️ Road Surface & Infrastructure Defects

Targets: Potholes, alligator cracking, road subsidence, surface ponding/waterlogging, damaged or obscured traffic signage, missing median dividers, and faded pedestrian zebra crossings.
Logic: Incorporates spatial bounding boxes, pixel-area segmentation, depth estimation, and accelerometer/gyro bump cross-validation.

🚗 Traffic Density & Flow Intelligence Flow

Targets: Vehicle classification (buses, cars, 2-wheelers, trucks, auto-rickshaws), volume counting, speed trajectory calculation via ByteTrack/DeepSORT, and queue length measurement.
Logic: Dynamically calculates corridor Level of Service (LOS A through F) and detects bottlenecks before systemic paralysis occurs.

🚨 Safety & Incident Anomaly Engine Safety

Targets: Near-miss collisions, abrupt deceleration spikes (>0.6g), erratic lane weaving, pedestrian intrusion into active roadways, and stationary stalled vehicles in live traffic lanes.
Logic: Multi-frame optical flow vectors combined with kinetic collision trajectory modeling.

📸 ANPR & Dedicated Lane Enforcement Enforcement

Targets: License plate recognition and unauthorized private vehicle intrusion into dedicated Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) corridors.
Logic: Two-stage detection (Plate BBox Localization → CRNN Character Recognition) with on-device blurring for compliant, non-violating vehicles.

4.2. Privacy by Design & Tamper-Evident Evidence Vault (Steps 30 & 31)

NovaFlow adheres strictly to data minimization standards and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act:

Privacy Mechanism Technical Implementation Regulatory Compliance
Data Minimization Faces and non-violating vehicle plates are blurred at the inference stage before leaving the bus. Prevents mass surveillance; zero storage of non-incident PII.
Ephemeral Ring Buffer Raw camera feeds reside in a volatile 30-second circular RAM buffer, continually overwritten. Eliminates continuous raw footage storage.
Cryptographic Custody When an incident is detected: Clip → SHA-256 Hash → UTC Timestamp → Audit Log → Secure Vault Tamper-evident, court-admissible evidence chain of custody.
Role-Based Access (RBAC) Granular permissions for ADMIN, TRAFFIC POLICE, ROAD ENGINEER, and ANALYST. Principle of least privilege across municipal stakeholders.

6. Closed-Loop Authority & Maintenance Lifecycle (Step 6)

NovaFlow bridges detection and physical maintenance through a finite state machine:

1. UNVERIFIED

Raw edge AI event logged. Placed in review queue for authority inspection.

2. CONFIRMED

Verified by operator or auto-confirmed by multi-bus consensus (>95% confidence).

3. UNDER REPAIR

Work order ticket generated (e.g. WO-2026-8412) and dispatched to road crews.

4. RESOLVED

Patch completed. Edge AI verifies road smoothness on subsequent bus passes.
